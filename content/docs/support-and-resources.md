If you need further assistance or have questions about the Fleetros Backoffice:

- **In-App Support:** configure your Support Contact in Settings > Account Settings to provide customers with your support email and phone number
- **Customer Portal:** direct your customers to portal.fleetros.com for self-service booking and account management
- **Feedback:** use the Post-Booking Discount settings to include a Feedback URL in automated customer communications

Thank you for choosing Fleetros.

We are committed to helping you run your rental business more efficiently.
