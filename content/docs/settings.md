**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/settings

The Settings page links to every configuration area of your Fleetros account. You can open each subsection from a card on this page, or go directly to its URL.

## Business Profile

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/settings/profile

Click Manage Profile from Settings, then click Edit Profile to update your Company Information: Company Name, Description, Email, Phone Number, Address, City, and Country.

This page also shows your Account Status (Account Type, Status, Member Since) and Payment Gateway onboarding progress (Onboarding, Account Status, Charges Pending, Payouts Pending, Gateway ID), with quick links to Payment Account Settings and Account Settings.

## Account Settings

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/settings/account-settings

Click Manage Settings from Settings. This page lets you configure:

- **Currency:** choose US Dollar, Malaysian Ringgit, Chinese Yuan, or Singapore Dollar
- **Booking Deposit:** choose Deposit Type (Percentage of total or Flat Amount) and set the Deposit Rate
- **Booking Reminder Notification:** set Hours Before Pickup (1 to 168 hours; default 24). Quick Select: 6, 24, 48, or 72 hours. Only affects new bookings.
- **Tax Rate:** set the tax rate applied to bookings
- **Service Fee Rate:** set the service fee rate applied to bookings
- **Support Contact:** set the Support Phone Number and Support Email Address shown to customers, then click Save Support Contact
- **Online Payment:** enable or disable online payments for customer bookings (requires completing Stripe onboarding first)
- **Payment Deadline:** enable a deadline after which unpaid bookings are automatically cancelled. Set the Deadline Duration in minutes (1 to 10,080; default 30). Quick Select: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 24 hours.
- **Post-Booking Discount:** enable automatic personalised discount codes sent after booking completion. Configure Discount Type (Percentage or Fixed Amount), Discount Value, Validity Period in days, and an optional Feedback URL, then click Save Post-Booking Discount Settings.

## Payment Account

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/settings/payment-account

1. Click Manage Payment Account from Settings.
2. Review your Payment Account Status, which shows Accept Payments and Receive Payouts indicators.
3. Click Continue Onboarding to complete your Stripe setup. All payments are processed through Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor.

## My Plan

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/settings/plan

1. Click Manage Plan from Settings.
2. Review your Current Plan details, including the plan name, price, billing cycle, and renewal date.
3. Click Change Plan to switch to a different subscription tier.
4. Under Included Features and Limits, review your usage caps (e.g. Bookings, Vehicles, Offerings, Team Members) and which optional features are enabled (e.g. Customer Segmentation, Custom Roles, Loyalty Program, Customer Portal).

## Modification Policies

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/settings/modification-policies

1. Click Manage Policies from Settings.
2. Click Create Policy to add a new modification policy.
3. View existing policies in the All Policies table, which lists the Policy Name, Tier, Free Window (e.g., 24h), Late Fee, Category Fee, and Location Fee for each policy.

## Inspection Checklists

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/settings/inspection-checklists

1. Click Manage Checklists from Settings.
2. Review the list of checklist templates used during vehicle delivery and retrieval (e.g., the Standard Vehicle Inspection template, which applies to both Delivery and Retrieval and contains 7 items).
3. Click New Template to create a new checklist, or Edit to modify an existing template.

## Loyalty Program

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/settings/loyalty

Click Manage Loyalty Tiers from Settings. The system includes four default tiers:

| Tier | Rentals/Year | Points Multiplier | Discount | Benefits |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Bronze | 0-4 | 1x | 0% | - |
| Silver | 5-9 | 1.25x | 0% | Priority |
| Gold | 10-19 | 1.5x | 5% | Priority, Upgrade |
| Platinum | 20+ | 2x | 10% | Priority, Upgrade, Guaranteed Availability |

Click Create Tier to add a custom tier, or View Customers to see which customers fall into each tier.

## Team Management

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/team

1. Click Manage Team from Settings.
2. Review Your Role and Permissions (e.g., Merchant Administrator with full access).
3. View the Team Members list, which shows each member's name, email, role, join date, and status.
4. Click Invite Team Member to add a new member to your account.

## Notification Settings

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/settings/notifications

Click Manage Notifications from Settings. Toggle individual notifications for the following events:

### Customer Notifications (sent by email)

- Booking Created, Booking Confirmed, Booking Modified, Booking Cancelled, Booking Completed
- Booking Reminder, Car Delivery Reminder, Car Pickup Reminder
- Car Delivered, Car Returned
- Payment Received, Payment Failed, Payment Refunded

### Merchant Notifications (sent by email and push)

- Same event types as above, delivered to your team

Changes save automatically. Click Reset to Defaults to restore the original notification settings.

## Customer Segmentation

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/settings/segmentation

1. Click Manage Segments from Settings.
2. Browse segment categories: Demographic, Geographic, Firmographic, Behavioural, and Psychographic.
3. Manage the classification values within each category (e.g., Young Adult 18-25, Adult 26-40, Middle-Aged 41-60, Senior 60+, Family with Kids, High Income).
4. Click Add Value to create a new classification, or Recalculate All to refresh behavioural segment assignments.

## Email Notes

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/settings/account-settings/email-notes

1. Click Manage Email Notes from Settings.
2. Configure custom rich-text notes shown in customer emails for Booking Reminder, Car Delivery Checklist, and Car Pickup Checklist. Each note can be enabled or disabled independently.
3. Click Create Note to add content using the rich text editor.

## Language and Theme

At the bottom of the Settings page, choose your interface Language (English, Chinese Simplified, or Bahasa Malaysia) and your display Theme (Light, Dark, or System), then click Save.
