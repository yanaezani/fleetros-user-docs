## Payments Tab

Click Payments in the sidebar. The Payments tab lists individual transactions with Payment ID, Amount, Method (e.g., Bank Transfer, Cash, Other), Type (Deposit or Final Settlement), Status, and Date.

Use the filter tabs: All Payments, By Booking, By Payer, By Date, By Status, By Method, or Advanced. A Simple View toggle hides non-cash entries such as write-offs and manual adjustments. Set Page Size and Sort options, then click Search, or Reset to clear filters.

## Settlements Tab

Click the Settlements tab to see outstanding balances across all bookings. This view displays four summary metrics:

- **Total Outstanding:** the total unpaid balance across all bookings
- **Open Settlements:** the number of bookings with remaining balances
- **High Balance:** the count of balances exceeding RM 1,000
- **Average Balance:** the average outstanding balance per booking

Below the summary, a table lists bookings with unpaid balances, sortable by Highest Balance, showing the Booking, Customer, Status, Balance, number of Transactions, and Last Activity date. Click Refresh to update the figures.

## Recording a Payment

Payments are recorded from within a specific booking:

1. Open the booking (Bookings > View).
2. Go to the Payments tab.
3. Click Record Payment to log a new payment against that booking.
4. Alternatively, use Write Off to clear an uncollectable balance, or Close Settlement once the booking is fully settled.

You can also click Print Receipt from the booking detail page to generate a printable payment receipt.
