## Viewing Discounts

Click Discounts in the sidebar to see all promotional codes. Use the filter tabs: All Discounts, By Code, By Type, By Status, By Value, By Scope, Valid Now, By Features, or Advanced. A Show Auto-Generated Codes toggle is also available. Set Page Size and Sort options, then click Search, or Reset to clear filters.

## Creating a New Discount

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/discounts/new

1. Click the + Create Discount button.
2. Under Discount Details, enter the Discount Code (required), Discount Status (Active, Inactive, or Expired), Discount Type (Percentage or Fixed Amount), and the discount value.
3. Under Applicability, choose the Scope: All entities, Package, Offering, or Booking, and optionally set a Minimum Booking Amount.
4. Under Usage Limits, set the Maximum Uses and view Current Uses.
5. Under Validity Period, set Valid From and Valid To dates, and an optional Description.
6. Under Standard Settings, choose whether the discount Requires Promo Code (customers must enter the code) and whether it is Combinable with Other Discounts.
7. Under Advanced Settings (available on paid plans), set a Priority (0-100, higher applied first), and choose whether to Auto-Apply the discount without a promo code, and whether to restrict it to First-Time Customers Only.
8. Click Create Discount to save, or Cancel to discard.
