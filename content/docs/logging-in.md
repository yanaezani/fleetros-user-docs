**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com

Fleetros uses single sign-on (SSO), so every team member logs in the same way.

## Sign In

1. Navigate to https://app.fleetros.com in your browser.
2. If you are not already signed in, you will automatically be redirected to the login screen.
3. Enter your account email and password on the sign-in screen, then submit the form.
4. After successful authentication, you will be redirected back to the Dashboard.

## Logging Out

1. Click the profile icon in the top-right corner of any page.
2. Select Logout from the dropdown menu.

The dropdown also shows your logged-in account email and a quick link to Settings.

> **Tip:** If you forget your password, use the password reset link on the login page.
