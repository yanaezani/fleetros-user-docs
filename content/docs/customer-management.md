**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/customers

## Viewing Customers

Click Customers in the sidebar to see all customer profiles. The list shows Name, Email, Phone, Gender, and a Completeness percentage indicating how much of their profile has been filled in.

## Viewing and Editing a Customer Profile

1. Click View on any customer row to open their profile page.
2. Review the Profile Completeness percentage and Segments assigned to the customer at the top of the page.

The customer profile includes the following sections:

- **Images:** upload a Profile Photo or Document (up to 10 images)
- **Identity:** update Email (note: changing the email also updates their sign-in username) and Phone
- **Personal Information:** update First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Gender, and Nationality
- **Identification:** record the ID Type and ID Number
- **Address:** record Street Address, City, State, Postal Code, and Country
- **Emergency Contact:** record a Contact Name and Contact Phone, plus general Notes (up to 1,000 characters)

When you are done, click Save to store your changes.
