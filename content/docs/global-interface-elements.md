These elements are available on every page of the Fleetros Backoffice:

## Sidebar Navigation

The left-hand sidebar provides quick access to all modules: Dashboard, Vehicles, Bookings, Accidents, Offerings, Discounts, Payments, Customers, Liability Options, and Settings. At the bottom of the sidebar, a Customer App link opens the separate customer-facing portal at portal.fleetros.com.

## Top Bar

- **Theme Toggle:** the moon/sun icon in the top-right of every page lets you quickly switch between light and dark mode
- **Notifications Bell:** click the bell icon to view account and system notifications
- **Profile Menu:** click the person icon to see your logged-in account email, with quick links to Settings and Logout

## Keyboard Shortcuts and Tips

- Use your browser's search (Ctrl+F / Cmd+F) to quickly find content on any list or detail page
- All filter searches support the Reset button to clear all criteria at once
- All list pages support Page Size controls to adjust how many items are shown per page
