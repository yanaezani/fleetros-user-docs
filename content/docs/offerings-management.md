Offerings are optional add-on services and products available to customers during booking, such as GPS, insurance, child seats, chauffeur service, accommodation, and more.

## Viewing Offerings

Click Offerings in the sidebar to see all configured offerings. Use the filter tabs: All Offerings, By Name, By Type, By Price, By Status, or Advanced Search. Set Page Size, Sort By, and Direction, then click Search, or Reset to clear filters.

## Adding a New Offering

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/offerings/new

1. Click the + Add Offering button.
2. Under Basic Information, enter the Offering Name (required), Offering Type (required: GPS, Insurance, Child Seat, Homestay, Villa, Chauffeur, Airport Pickup, Full Tank, Toll Pass, or Other), and an optional Description.
3. Under Inventory Settings, choose an Inventory Mode: Shared (multiple bookings can overlap, tracked by quantity) or Exclusive (one customer at a time). Then choose a Consumable Type: Returnable (e.g. GPS or child seat), Consumable, Service, or Accommodation.
4. Under Pricing and Availability, enter the Unit Price, Available Units, and Maximum Quantity per Booking, and optionally mark the offering as Mandatory for all bookings.
5. Under Offering Pricing Rules, add one or more rules. Each rule has a Rate Type (Daily, Hourly, Fixed, or Per Rental), Base Rate, Priority (higher values take precedence), and Status (Active, Default, or Fallback). You can optionally set a Minimum/Maximum Quantity, a Never Expires toggle or Valid From/To dates, and a Description. Click Add Another Pricing Rule for more rate options.
6. Click Create New Offering to save, or Cancel to discard.
