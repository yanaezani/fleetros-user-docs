## Viewing Accident Reports

Click Accidents in the sidebar to see all accident reports across your fleet. The table lists ID, Date, Vehicle, Customer, Severity, Status, Insurance status, and Repair status.

Use the filter tabs: All, By Severity, By Status, By Insurance, By Repair, By Date Range, By Booking, By Vehicle, or Advanced. Set Per Page and Sort options, then click Search, or Reset to clear filters.

## Creating a New Accident Report

1. Click the + New Report button.
2. Under Booking and Vehicle, enter the Booking ID first; the Vehicle field then becomes available to select the specific vehicle tied to that booking.
3. Under Accident Information, enter the Accident Date (required) and Location. Choose a Severity (Minor, Major, or Total Loss; defaults to Minor) and Fault Type (defaults to Undetermined). You can also add a free-text Description, and optionally a Police Report number and Estimated Damage Cost.
4. Under Third Party, tick Third Party Involved if applicable.
5. Under Person In Charge, optionally record the Name, Phone, Email, Office, Office Phone, and Office Address of the person handling the case.
6. Under Insurance, optionally record the Insurance Company and Policy number.
7. Click Create Report to save.

## Viewing and Managing an Accident Report

Click View on any accident row to open its detail page. The page header shows the report severity and current status, with Edit and Delete actions. Four tabs are available:

### Details Tab

Shows Accident Status, Insurance Status, and Repair Status at a glance, followed by full Accident Information, Booking and Vehicle details, Person In Charge, Insurance details (including Policy number, Claim number, and Insurance Company), and Repair information (including Repair Status and Damage Costs). It also shows when the report was created and last updated, and by whom.

### Images Tab

Upload and view photos related to the accident, including vehicle damage photos, scene photos, and any supporting documentation.

### History Tab

A complete audit trail of all changes made to the accident report, including status updates, insurance claim changes, and repair progress.

### Financials Tab

Tracks all costs associated with the accident, including repair costs, insurance claims, and any customer charges.
