Fleetros is a web-based platform for managing your vehicle rental fleet. It gives you one dashboard to run your entire rental business from any browser.

This guide walks you through every feature of Fleetros, from logging in to using each management tool.

## Key Features

- **Vehicle Management:** register vehicles, track statuses, configure pricing, and manage fleet inventory
- **Booking Management:** create and manage reservations with multi-vehicle support, modification workflows, and hand-over tracking
- **Financial Tools:** record payments, track settlements, manage discounts, and generate revenue reports
- **Customer Management:** maintain customer profiles, assign segments, and track loyalty tiers
- **Accident Reporting:** log incidents, track insurance claims, monitor repair progress, and record costs
- **Offerings and Add-ons:** configure optional services like GPS, insurance, chauffeur, and accommodation packages
- **Liability Reduction Options:** define damage-liability tiers with daily rates, excess fees, and coverage benefits
- **Team Collaboration:** invite team members, assign roles, and manage permissions
- **Configurable Settings:** customise business profile, notification preferences, inspection checklists, loyalty tiers, modification policies, and more

## System Requirements

- Any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- Stable internet connection
- Screen resolution of 1280 x 720 or higher recommended
