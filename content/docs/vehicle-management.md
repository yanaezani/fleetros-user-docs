## Viewing Your Vehicles

1. Click Vehicles in the left sidebar to see your fleet list. Each vehicle card shows a photo, name, status, license plate, category, seat count, fuel type, transmission, and odometer reading.
2. Above the list, use the filter tabs to narrow your search: All Vehicles, By Name, By Make, By Model, By License Plate, By Status, By Category, By Seating, or Advanced Search.
3. Selecting By Status shows a dropdown with Available, Rented, Maintenance, and Unavailable. You can also set Page Size, Sort By, and Order, then click Search. Click Reset to clear all filters.

## Adding a New Vehicle

**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/vehicles/new

1. Click the + Add Vehicle button (top right) to open the 5-step wizard. You can click Save Draft at any step to save your progress.
2. Under Vehicle Information, fill in the Vehicle Name and License Plate (both required). Set the Status (defaults to Available) and optionally add the VIN and Odometer reading (defaults to 0).
3. Under Specifications, enter the Make, Model, Year, and Number of Seats (all required). Choose a Fuel Type (defaults to Gasoline), Vehicle Category (e.g. Sedan, SUV, Van), and Transmission Type (defaults to Automatic).
4. Under Rental Settings, set Buffer Minutes (blocked time before/after each booking, default 30), Min Rental Hours (default 24), Max Rental Days (default 30), and Max Future Booking Days (default 90).
5. Under Additional Details, enter an optional Description with any extra information about the vehicle.
6. Under Pricing Configuration, choose a Rate Type (Hourly, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Flat Rate), set the Base Rate and Minimum Rental Days, then either toggle Never Expires or set Valid From/To dates. You can mark this as the default daily pricing and click Add Another Pricing for more rate options. At least one pricing entry is required.
7. Click Create Vehicle to save and add the vehicle to your fleet, or Previous to go back and adjust earlier steps.

## Vehicle Statuses

| Status | Description |
| :---- | :---- |
| Available | The vehicle is ready to be booked by customers. |
| Rented | The vehicle is currently out on a booking. |
| Maintenance | The vehicle is undergoing service or repair. |
| Out of Service | The vehicle has been taken out of the active fleet (labelled Retired on the Edit Vehicle screen). |

## Editing a Vehicle

1. From the Vehicles list, click View Details on any vehicle card to open its detail page, which shows Vehicle Images (with upload and primary-image options), Pricing, Basic Information, Specifications, and Rental Settings.
2. Click Edit at the top of the detail page (or Delete to remove the vehicle).
3. The Edit Vehicle page shows all fields on one scrollable page: Basic Information, Specifications, Rental Settings, and Additional Details on the left, plus a Vehicle Pricing panel on the right where you can add new pricing.
4. Update any fields as needed, then click Update Vehicle to save your changes, or Cancel to discard them.
