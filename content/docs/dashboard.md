**URL:** https://app.fleetros.com/dashboard

The Dashboard is the landing page after login and gives an at-a-glance summary of your fleet business.

## Statistics Overview

The top of the Dashboard displays four key metrics, each compared against the previous 30 days:

- **Total Vehicles:** the number of vehicles registered in your fleet
- **Active Bookings:** currently active reservations
- **Total Revenue:** cumulative revenue for the period
- **Active Offerings:** the number of add-on services currently available

Click Refresh to update the figures with the latest data.

## Revenue Report

The Revenue Report panel lets you generate financial summaries for any period:

1. Select a Report Type (e.g., Monthly).
2. Choose the Year and Month.
3. Click Generate to calculate the report.

Use the Current Report tab to view the most recently generated report, and the Historical Reports tab to browse previously generated reports.

## Revenue Breakdown

Below the report panel, the Revenue Breakdown section shows the report period and a breakdown of income by category:

- **Vehicle Rentals:** income from base vehicle rental charges
- **Damage Liability:** income from liability reduction option add-ons
- **Offerings:** income from optional add-on services

Each category displays its amount and percentage of the total revenue.
